Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

This website (trovemart.co.uk) is operated by Momin Khan Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom, trading as TroveMart. This policy explains what limited information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order on this website we receive your name, email address and delivery address, and details of what you ordered and what you paid. We need these to take your order, deliver it and handle any return.

We never receive or store your card details. Card payments are taken by Stripe, who process them on their own secure pages under their own privacy policy. All we are told is that the payment succeeded, along with the order details above.

We also receive personal information if you choose to contact us — your name, email address and the message you send us by email, through our contact form or on WhatsApp.

If you buy through our eBay store instead, eBay handles that order and your information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years, which is how long UK tax law requires us to keep records of a sale. Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted once they are dealt with.

Who we share it with

Only where it is needed to complete your order: Stripe (to take payment) and the delivery company carrying your parcel. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for marketing.

Cookies & tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run third-party analytics that identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To help resolve questions about an order placed through our eBay store.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email info@mominkhanltd.com.

Third parties

When you buy from us you are directed to eBay, and your purchase is subject to eBay’s own User Privacy Notice. Please review it to understand how eBay processes your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
