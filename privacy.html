Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

This website (trovemart.co.uk) is operated by Momin Khan Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom, trading as TroveMart. This policy explains what limited information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

This website is a product catalogue. It does not take payments, create customer accounts, or store your card details. Purchases are completed on eBay, which handles your order and payment under its own privacy policy.

We only receive personal information if you choose to contact us — for example, your name, email address and the message you send us by email or via our contact form.

Cookies & tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run third-party analytics that identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To help resolve questions about an order placed through our eBay store.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email info@mominkhanltd.com.

Third parties

When you buy from us you are directed to eBay, and your purchase is subject to eBay’s own User Privacy Notice. Please review it to understand how eBay processes your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@mominkhanltd.com.
