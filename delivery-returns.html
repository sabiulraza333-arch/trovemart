Delivery & Returns

Dispatched from the UK — with your full consumer rights, clearly set out.

There are two ways to buy from us, and your rights differ slightly. Orders placed on this website are sold to you directly by Momin Khan Ltd and are covered by UK consumer law, set out below. Orders placed through our eBay store follow eBay’s own process and are covered by the eBay Money Back Guarantee.

Delivery

  • Items are dispatched from the UK, normally within 1–2 working days of your order.
  • UK delivery is free — the price you see is the price you pay. There are no extra charges at checkout.
  • We will deliver within 30 days of your order at the latest, unless we have agreed something different with you.
  • If anything delays your order, we will contact you by email.

Changed your mind? 14-day cancellation

If you bought on this website, you may cancel for any reason — you do not have to give a reason.

  • You have 14 days from the day you receive the goods to tell us you are cancelling.
  • Tell us by email at info@mominkhanltd.com with your order number. You may use the cancellation wording below, but you do not have to.
  • Once you have told us, you then have a further 14 days to send the goods back.
  • We refund within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of you showing proof that you have sent them, whichever is sooner. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
  • Because delivery is free, we refund the full price you paid for the goods.
  • Return postage for a change of mind is paid by you.
  • We may reduce your refund if the goods have been handled more than was necessary to check them — in the way you could in a shop.

Cancellation wording you can copy: “I hereby give notice that I cancel my contract of sale of the following goods: [item], ordered on [date], order number [number]. Name, address, date.”

Faulty, damaged or not as described

This is separate from changing your mind, and your rights are stronger.

  • Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.
  • If they are not, you have 30 days from delivery to reject them for a full refund.
  • After 30 days we will repair or replace the item; if that is not possible, you may claim a refund.
  • We pay the return postage on anything faulty, damaged or wrongly described.
  • Please email us photos if you can — it usually lets us resolve things the same day.

These rights come from the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Nothing on this page affects your statutory rights.

Items that cannot be returned for a change of mind

By law, the 14-day cancellation right does not apply to a few things — for example sealed health, hygiene or cosmetic products once the seal is broken, or goods made or personalised to your specification. Your rights for faulty goods are unaffected.

How to return something

  1. Email info@mominkhanltd.com with your order number and whether the item is faulty or you have changed your mind.
  2. We reply with the return address and confirm who pays the postage.
  3. Please get a free proof of postage — until the parcel reaches us, it is your responsibility.

Please do not send returns to our office address without emailing first. The office does not hold stock and cannot process returns.

If you bought through our eBay store

Orders placed on eBay are handled under eBay’s process, not this page. Start a return from your eBay Purchase History, and your order is covered by the eBay Money Back Guarantee. Delivery cost and return terms for those orders are shown on each eBay listing.
