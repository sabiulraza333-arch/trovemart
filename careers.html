How we hire

No tricks, no ghosting.

01

We read every application ourselves. You will get a reply either way, normally within a week.

02

A short, friendly conversation — on the phone or at the office — about the work and about you.

03

An offer

If it is a fit, we make an offer in writing with the pay and hours set out clearly.
